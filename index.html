About the Author

Rachit Ratnam Mudaliar

I am a Software Systems student at Kauno Kolegija in Kaunas, Lithuania, and a freelance app developer. I have been fascinated by technology since childhood, and I build offline-first apps for Android and iOS.

This is where I write about what I learn: how the internet works, how software is built, and the ideas behind the tools we use every day. Every article is based on reliable sources, listed at the end of each piece.
