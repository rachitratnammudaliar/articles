From the Editor

A New Publication on the Technology We Use Every Day

Short, carefully researched articles that explain how the internet, software and modern devices really work, written for everyone, not only engineers.

By Rachit Ratnam MudaliarKaunas, Lithuania

Kaunas — Every day we send messages, watch videos and open websites without thinking about what happens behind the screen. The technology works so well that it feels almost invisible. Yet behind every click there are real machines, real cables and real engineering decisions.

This publication exists to make that hidden world visible. Each article takes one topic, such as how the internet crosses oceans or how an app keeps working offline, and explains it step by step in plain English.

“The internet is not in the clouds. It is in glass, under our streets and on the ocean floor.”

Every article follows three simple rules. It must be clear enough for any reader. It must be based on reliable sources, listed at the end of the piece. And it must explain the important technical terms, so that readers finish with new words they can use.

The first article, on the physical backbone of the internet, is being prepared now and will appear on this front page soon.
